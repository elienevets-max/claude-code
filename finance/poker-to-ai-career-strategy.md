## 1. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (This Week)

### A. The Crypto vs. Debt Decision

**Sell $5,000 of crypto immediately. Pay off the credit card.**

Reasoning in poker terms: carrying credit card debt at 20-28% APR while holding a volatile asset is like calling off your stack with a gutshot when you're already short-stacked. The math doesn't work.

- Credit card interest at ~24% APR = $100/month in pure rake going to the house
- Crypto could go up. It could also go down 40% in a week. You can't afford that variance with zero cash on hand
- After payoff: $25,000 in crypto, $0 debt, $0 cash

**Then sell another $10,000 of crypto into cash.** This gives you:
- $10,000 cash (roughly 3 months of bare minimum survival)
- $20,000 in crypto (still exposed to upside)
- $0 debt

This is your new bankroll. Treat it like one.

**Framework reference:** Psychology of Money (`finance/psychology-of-money.md`) — "Getting wealthy and staying wealthy are opposite skills." Right now you need the staying-wealthy skill: humility, frugality, paranoia, defense. Survival mindset over growth mindset.

### B. Cut the Burn Rate — Now

Your $3,400/month burn is too high for your bankroll.

| Expense | Current | Target | Savings |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| Rent + bills | $2,400 | Find a roommate or negotiate rent down. Target $1,800 | $600 |
| Car + insurance | $1,000 | Can you downgrade insurance? Sell the car for something cheaper? Target $700 | $300 |
| **Total** | **$3,400** | **$2,500** | **$900/month** |

At $2,500/month burn with $10K cash, you have 4 months of runway. With crypto as emergency backup, you have closer to 12 months. That changes the game.

**Framework reference:** Personal Finance Blueprint (`finance/personal-finance-blueprint.md`) — Ramit's Conscious Spending Plan. Your fixed costs need to be under 60% of income. Right now they're 100%+ because you have no stable income.

### C. The Poker Stake Decision

**Take the stake. But with conditions.**

Here's why: you need cash flow while you build. The stake at 55/45 (you keep 55%) is essentially a job with variable pay. The downside — going into makeup — is real, but here's the calculus:

- If you play disciplined at stakes you beat (2/5, 5/10 — games where your edge is clear), the probability of running bad enough to go deep into makeup over 30 days is manageable
- The 45% you're giving up is insurance premium against going broke
- This generates $3,000-$8,000/month if you're playing well, even at lower stakes

**Rules for the stake:**
1. Play 20-25 hours/week max (not 40+). You need the other hours for building
2. Stay at stakes where your edge is clearest — this isn't the time for shot-taking
3. Set a 30-day review: if you're in makeup after 100+ hours, reassess
4. Treat the poker income as bridge money, not career money

**Poker-to-business translation:** This is bankroll management 101. You're playing within your roll while simultaneously building a second income stream. The stake removes the risk of ruin from your immediate plan.

#### Automation #1: AI-Powered Customer Service & Lead Capture Bot

**What it does:** A chatbot on kingofsparklers.com that handles the 80% of repetitive questions (product recommendations based on event type, bulk pricing inquiries, shipping timelines, safety certifications) and captures lead info for B2B prospects.

**Business pain it solves:**
- Your brother or his team answers the same 15-20 questions repeatedly
- B2B leads (nightclubs, event planners, hotels) often bounce from the site because they can't get immediate answers about bulk pricing or customization
- After Crans-Montana, safety questions are spiking — every venue wants to know about certifications, and a bot can instantly deliver safety documentation

**The MVP (buildable in 1-2 weeks):**
1. Chatbot trained on King of Sparklers' product catalog, pricing tiers, and FAQ
2. Handles: "What sparklers work for weddings?", "What's the bulk price for 100 cases?", "Are these safe for indoor use?", "Do you have safety certifications?"
3. When the question gets complex or the visitor is a B2B lead → captures name, email, event type, and routes to your brother or his team
4. Tracks every question asked (data goldmine for understanding what customers want)

**Estimated impact:**
- Saves 5-10 hours/week of repetitive customer service
- Captures 20-30% more B2B leads that currently bounce (these leads are worth hundreds to thousands per order)
- Safety question handling positions KoS as the knowledgeable, trustworthy brand
