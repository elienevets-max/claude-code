**Estimated time to first dollar:** 14-21 days. You can build the pipeline in a weekend, land a first client through your Las Vegas network or cold outreach within two weeks.

**Difficulty rating:** 3/10 — Low technical barrier with your skills; main challenge is client acquisition.

**Founder's Triangle check:** Domain ✅ (content + social) | Depth ✅ (AI pipelines) | Distribution ✅ (local network + social media knowledge)

## 2-Week Validation Sprint: AI-Powered Content Repurposing Agency

### Sprint Objective

By Day 14, you will have: (a) a working AI content repurposing pipeline, (b) a portfolio sample, (c) a clear offer page, and (d) at least 2-3 live conversations with potential clients. Target: first paying client signed by end of sprint.

---

### Week 1: Build the Machine

#### Day 1-2: Pipeline Architecture

**Goal:** Build a working AI pipeline that takes one long-form input and produces multi-platform outputs.

- [ ] Define input formats you'll accept (podcast audio, YouTube video URL, blog post, keynote transcript)
- [ ] Build the Claude-powered extraction pipeline:
  - Input → full transcript (use Whisper or existing transcript)
  - Transcript → key themes, quotes, insights, frameworks (structured extraction)
  - Structured data → platform-specific outputs:
    - 5 TikTok/Reels script hooks + scripts (under 60 seconds)
    - 5 Twitter/X threads
    - 3 LinkedIn posts (thought leadership style)
    - 2 email newsletter sections
    - 10 quote graphics (text for Canva templates)
    - 1 blog post (SEO-optimized)
- [ ] Test pipeline end-to-end with one piece of content
- [ ] Document the workflow so it's repeatable

#### Day 3-4: Portfolio Sample + Offer Design

**Goal:** Create a compelling "before and after" sample and a clear offer.

- [ ] Pick a public podcast episode or YouTube video from a known creator
- [ ] Run it through your pipeline — produce the full content package
- [ ] Create a visual "what you get" breakdown (can use Canva or Gamma)
- [ ] Design your offer structure:
  - **Starter:** 1 long-form piece → 15+ content pieces/week — $1,500/month
  - **Growth:** 2 long-form pieces → 30+ content pieces/week — $2,500/month
  - **Scale:** 4 long-form pieces → 60+ content pieces/week — $4,000/month
- [ ] Build a simple landing page (Carrd, Framer, or single Notion page) with:
  - The problem (you create great long-form content but it dies after one use)
  - The solution (we turn every piece into 30+ platform-native content assets)
  - The proof (your portfolio sample)
  - The offer (packages + pricing)
  - The CTA (book a 15-minute call)

#### Day 5: Outreach List Building

**Goal:** Identify 50 potential clients who need this service right now.

- [ ] Identify the ideal client profile:
  - Creates regular long-form content (podcast, YouTube, webinars, keynotes)
  - Has 1,000-100,000 followers (big enough to care about content, small enough to not have a team)
  - In a monetizable niche (business, finance, health, real estate, coaching)
- [ ] Build a list of 50 targets:
  - 15 from your Las Vegas local network
  - 15 from podcast directories (search for podcasts with good content but weak social presence)
  - 10 from YouTube (search for channels uploading regularly but not repurposing)
  - 10 from LinkedIn (thought leaders posting long-form but not on short-form platforms)
- [ ] For each target, note: their name, content type, audience size, what's missing from their content strategy, and the best way to reach them

---

### Week 2: Sell and Validate

#### Day 6-7: Free Sample Outreach (First 10 Targets)

**Goal:** Send personalized, value-first outreach to 10 prospects.

- [ ] For each of the first 10 targets, do this:
  1. Take their most recent piece of long-form content
  2. Run it through your pipeline
  3. Pick the 3 best output pieces
  4. Send a personalized message: *"Hey [Name], I love your [podcast/video] on [topic]. I actually ran it through my AI content system and created these 3 pieces for you — here they are, no strings attached. If you want, I can show you what the full 30+ piece package looks like. Either way, hope these are useful."*
- [ ] Track responses in a simple spreadsheet (name, date sent, response, status)

**Why this works:** You're leading with value, not a pitch. The sample does the selling. This is the "Credit Card Test" from the Business Validation Playbook — but instead of asking "would you pay for this?", you're showing them what they'd be paying for.

#### Day 8-9: Follow-Up + Expand Outreach

**Goal:** Follow up with initial 10, launch outreach to next 20.

- [ ] Follow up with non-responders from the first batch (one follow-up, not pushy)
- [ ] For the next 20 targets, use a slightly faster approach:
  - Create a 60-second Loom video showing a content repurposing transformation
  - Send the video + a brief message offering a free sample for their specific content
- [ ] Book discovery calls with anyone who responds positively
- [ ] Post your portfolio sample on LinkedIn and Twitter with a breakdown of the process (meta: your content about content repurposing IS content marketing)

#### Day 10-11: Discovery Calls + Closing

**Goal:** Convert interest into paying clients.

- [ ] On discovery calls, follow this structure:
  1. **Understand:** What content do they create? How often? What happens to it after publishing?
  2. **Diagnose:** Where are they leaving money on the table? (Usually: great content that reaches 10% of potential audience)
  3. **Prescribe:** Show the full package output from your pipeline. Walk through each piece.
  4. **Offer:** Present the package that matches their volume. Offer a 30-day trial at full price with a money-back guarantee.
- [ ] If they push back on price, offer a smaller pilot: "Let me repurpose your next 2 episodes. If you don't see the value, you pay nothing."
- [ ] Get at least one signed client or one paid pilot by end of Day 11

#### Day 12-13: Deliver + Optimize

**Goal:** Deliver exceptional first output and refine the pipeline.

- [ ] Deliver the first full content package to your paying client
- [ ] Ask for specific feedback: What's most useful? What needs adjustment? What platforms matter most to them?
- [ ] Iterate on the pipeline based on real-world feedback
- [ ] Document what worked and what didn't in your outreach (conversion rates, response rates, common objections)

#### Day 14: Sprint Retrospective + Decision

**Goal:** Make a go/no-go decision with real data.

- [ ] Answer these questions honestly:
  - Did you get a paying client? (Strong go signal)
  - Did you get strong positive responses even without a sale yet? (Moderate go signal)
  - Did nobody care despite 30+ personalized outreaches? (Pivot signal)
- [ ] If GO: Map out the next 30 days — client acquisition targets, pipeline improvements, capacity planning
- [ ] If PIVOT: Review the response data. What resonated? What didn't? Use insights to refine the offer or pivot to Idea #2 or #3 from the ranked list
- [ ] Document the entire sprint — this documentation becomes content for marketing the service (meta-level content repurposing)

### Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| "AI content sounds robotic" | Your pipeline includes human-style editing prompts. You review and polish every output. Position as "AI-assisted, human-refined." |
| "Client expects perfection on Day 1" | Set clear expectations: first delivery is calibration. Quality increases each week as the system learns their voice. |
| "Can't find clients fast enough" | Lean on Las Vegas network first — warm intros convert 10x faster than cold outreach. |

| "Pricing pushback" | Compare to the alternative: hiring a social media manager ($4,000-$6,000/month) or a freelance content team ($3,000-$5,000/month). You're the budget option with better output. |
