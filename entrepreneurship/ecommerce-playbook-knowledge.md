## Overview
Comprehensive ecommerce growth strategy synthesized from 9 business books into 4 pillars with 10 actionable frameworks and a 90-day execution plan.

## The 4 Pillars

### Pillar 1: Customer Acquisition Engine
- **Framework 1: Bullseye Method** (Traction/Weinberg) - Test 19 growth channels systematically, focus on top 3
- **Framework 2: Customer Value Journey** (Ecommerce Evolved/Larsson) - 8 stages from Awareness to Promotion
- **Framework 3: Dream 100** (Ultimate Sales Machine/Holmes) - Target 100 transformative accounts with persistent outreach

### Pillar 2: Brand Positioning & Messaging
- **Framework 4: Own One Word** (Positioning/Ries & Trout) - Own "premium event sparklers" in customer's mind
- **Framework 5: StoryBrand BrandScript** (Building a StoryBrand/Miller) - Customer = hero, Brand = guide, 7-part messaging framework

### Pillar 3: B2B Sales Systems
- **Framework 6: Predictable Revenue Model** (Ross) - Seeds/Nets/Spears lead types, 8-touch outreach sequence
- **Framework 7: Education-Based Selling** (Holmes) - "5 Biggest Mistakes" framework positions you as expert

### Pillar 4: Operations & Scaling
- **Framework 8: Franchise Prototype** (E-Myth/Gerber) - Systemize with SOPs so anyone can run the business
- **Framework 9: EOS 6 Components** (Traction/Wickman) - Vision, People, Data, Issues, Process, Traction + quarterly Rocks
- **Framework 10: Clockwork 4D** (Michalowicz) - Doing, Deciding, Delegating, Designing + seasonal pre-loading

## 90-Day Execution Timeline
- **Month 1 (Weeks 1-4):** Foundation — Dream 100 list, Bullseye scoring, StoryBrand BrandScript, lead magnet, SOP documentation
- **Month 2 (Weeks 5-8):** Launch — B2B outreach begins, website overhaul, channel testing results, education marketing
- **Month 3 (Weeks 9-12):** Optimize — Pipeline review, customer value journey build-out, delegation audit, quarterly Rock review

## Key Metrics (Weekly Scorecard)
- Website visitors (grow 10%/month)
- Email list size (grow 50/week)
- Lead magnet downloads (20+/week)
- B2C conversion rate (2-4%)
- Average order value (increase 10%/quarter)
- Dream 100 contacts made (10/week)
- B2B pipeline value (grow monthly)
- New B2B accounts (5-10/month)
- Rocks on track (80%+)
- Owner hours worked (trending toward 40/week)
