## Before You Send: Research Checklist

For each planner on the Dream 25 list, spend 10 minutes gathering:

- [ ] Review their Instagram for recent weddings (note a specific venue, detail, or style)
- [ ] Check their website portfolio for their aesthetic (luxury, rustic, modern, etc.)
- [ ] See if they already feature sparkler exits in any photos
- [ ] Find the lead planner's first name
- [ ] Note their primary city and typical venue types

This research fuels the personalization that makes these emails work. Generic outreach gets deleted.

## Email 1: The Value Lead

**Send:** Day 1
**Goal:** Open the relationship with something useful — no pitch, no ask.

**Subject:** Quick sparkler tip for {{companyName}}'s summer weddings

---

Hi {{plannerName}},

I came across {{recentWeddingDetail}} on your Instagram — stunning work.

One thing we've learned from supplying sparklers for 10,000+ events: the #1 mistake at sparkler exits is lighting all the sparklers at once. Half burn out before the couple walks through.

The fix is simple — light in waves, back-to-front, and signal the couple 15-30 seconds after the last sparkler is lit. Every sparkler is at peak brightness when they walk through.

Small detail, big difference in photos.

Best,
[Your Name]
King of Sparklers

---

*Word count: 96*
*Strategy: Education-Based Selling — teach something valuable before asking for anything. This positions KoS as the expert, not a vendor. No CTA in this email.*

## Email 2: The Social Proof

**Send:** Day 4 (3 days after Email 1)
**Goal:** Build credibility through a peer's success story.

**Subject:** How one planner added sparkler packages to 200+ weddings

---

Hi {{plannerName}},

Quick follow-up — wanted to share something relevant.

One of the wedding planners we work with built sparkler exits into her standard packages for every wedding. She orders 36-inch sparklers in bulk for a 3-4 minute burn time — plenty of time for the photographer to get multiple angles.

Her sparkler exit photos became her most-tagged content on Instagram. Couples started requesting it specifically.

I put together a short Sparkler Exit Guide with setup tips, timing, and photo advice — happy to send it over if useful.

Best,
[Your Name]
King of Sparklers

---

*Word count: 103*
*Strategy: StoryBrand Authority — social proof from a peer planner is more persuasive than any company claim. The offer to send the exit guide is a Transitional CTA (low commitment, high value).*

## Email 3: The Resource Drop

**Send:** Day 9 (5 days after Email 2)
**Goal:** Deliver a tangible resource that demonstrates expertise.

**Subject:** Wedding sparkler cheat sheet — 10" vs 20" vs 36"

---

Hi {{plannerName}},

Whether or not my last email landed, I thought this might be useful for your team.

I put together a one-page cheat sheet comparing wedding sparkler sizes — burn times, guest count recommendations, and a quick formula for how many sparklers per wedding (1 per guest + 10% buffer).

Here's the quick math: a 150-person wedding needs about 165 sparklers. With 20-inch sparklers, that runs roughly $140-$180 total — a small line item that creates the highlight photo of the night.

Happy to send a sample kit to your office so you can see the quality firsthand. What's the best address?

Best,
[Your Name]
King of Sparklers

---

*Word count: 120*
*Strategy: Transitional CTA — not asking for the sale, asking for permission to deliver more value (the sample kit). Attaching the Product Cheat Sheet with this email gives them something to reference even if they don't reply.*

- **Dedicated support** — a direct line, not a contact form
- **Sample kits** for your office so couples can see the product in person

Wedding season kicks off May 1, and the planners who lock in vendors by March have the smoothest season. Would 15 minutes this week work for a quick call to see if this makes sense?

Best,
[Your Name]
King of Sparklers
1-800-913-5122

---

*Word count: 131*
*Strategy: Direct CTA — after three value touches, this is the ask. Specific time frame ("15 minutes this week") reduces perceived effort. Listing benefits in bullet format makes it scannable. The seasonal urgency is real, not manufactured.*

## How This Sequence Differs from General Outreach

The existing King of Sparklers 8-touch sequence covers nightclubs, hotels, event companies, and wedding planners. This 5-touch sequence is tailored specifically for wedding planners:

| Element | General 8-Touch | Wedding Planner 5-Touch |
|---------|----------------|------------------------|
| Length | 8 weeks | 23 days |
| Channels | Email + LinkedIn + phone + mail | Email only (planners live in their inbox) |
| Tone | Corporate B2B | Personal, peer-to-peer |
| Social proof | ROI stats (15-20% revenue increase) | Portfolio/Instagram angle (tagged photos) |
| Lead magnet | General lookbook | Sparkler Exit Guide + Product Cheat Sheet |
| Primary CTA | Discovery call | Sample kit → call |

## Re-Engagement Strategy

For planners who didn't convert during the initial sequence:

- **October:** Send a "How was your season?" email. Ask for feedback even if they didn't buy.
- **November:** Share a year-end industry insight or trend piece.
- **January:** Restart a modified sequence for the next wedding season.

The average B2B sale happens after 5-12 touches. This first sequence is touches 1-5. The relationship continues.
