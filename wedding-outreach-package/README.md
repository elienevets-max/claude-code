## How to Execute

**Step 1 — Review the Dream 25 list.** Prioritize the 12 "High" priority targets for the first wave. Verify contact info is current.

**Step 2 — Import contacts into the Dream 100 CRM.** Set category to "Wedding" and status to "Prospect."

**Step 3 — Launch the email sequence on March 1.** Start with the High priority targets. Stagger Medium priority targets one week later. Personalize each email using the research notes in the Dream 25 list.

**Step 4 — Use the supporting assets as attachments.** Include the Sparkler Exit Guide with Email 2 and the Product Cheat Sheet with Email 3.

**Step 5 — Track and follow up.** Log every touch in the CRM following the B2B Dream 100 Outreach SOP. Ship sample kits to interested planners within 48 hours of request.
