## When to Recommend Each Size

### 10-Inch — The Quick Moment
- Table sparklers during the reception
- Intimate elopements or micro-weddings (under 50 guests)
- Cake-adjacent photo moments
- Budget-conscious couples who still want the sparkler aesthetic

### 20-Inch — The Go-To Standard
- **Most popular for sparkler exits** — gives the couple a full 2+ minutes to walk through
- Ideal for indoor venues (smokeless, ashless)
- Works for any guest count from 50 to 200
- Best balance of burn time, brightness, and cost

### 36-Inch — The Grand Statement
- Premium sparkler exits at luxury venues
- Extra-long burn time means more photo and video opportunities
- Bright, dramatic gold flame for professional photography
- The "wow factor" choice for couples who want the best
