How to Use

  1. Click "Start Recording" — your browser will ask you to share a tab
  2. Select THIS tab and make sure "Share tab audio" is checked
  3. The YouTube video will auto-play — audio is being recorded
  4. When the lesson ends, click "Stop & Save" to download the audio file
  5. Click "Next" to move to the next lesson and repeat
  6. After recording all lessons, run: bash tools/transcribe-recordings.sh
