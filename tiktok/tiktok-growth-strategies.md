# TikTok Growth & Selling Mastery: Skills and Strategies

## Source: Podcast with Kent Yoshimura (Co-founder/CEO of Neurogum — TikTok's fastest growing brand of 2024)

---

## Core Skills You Need

### 1. Consistency & Volume (The #1 Skill)

- Post every single day. Neurogum's head of growth and CEO committed to daily posting starting Jan 1, 2024.
- Don't hesitate. "Fear is a mind killer. Just go."
- Focus on inputs first, not outputs. Spend 6+ months just posting, iterating, and learning the platform before expecting results.
- Speed and quantity matter more than production quality in the current landscape.

### 2. Hook Creation & Testing

- Maintain a spreadsheet/database of hooks to systematically test.
- Understand what a hook is: the opening that captures attention and resonates with the consumer.
- A/B test everything — hooks, value props, talking points — and track micrometrics (views, watch time, click-throughs).
- Winning hook categories from Neurogum's experience:
  - Underdog/comeback stories (e.g., Shark Tank rejection turned success)
  - Celebrity/authority endorsements reframed as content (e.g., Joe Rogan clips)
  - Media appearances repackaged as credibility-building content
- Iterate on hooks that show traction. Feed winning hooks to creators and let them build on top.

### 3. Platform Fluency (Learn By Doing)

- You must personally understand how TikTok works before outsourcing or delegating.
- Learn the algorithm, content formats, trends, and creator dynamics firsthand.
- If you don't know how the platform works, you cannot direct creators or agencies effectively.
- Build everything in-house first. Agencies and TikTok affiliate partners (TAPs/TSPs) have limits — they all do the same thing and hit saturation.

### 4. Creator Cultivation & Community Building

This is the highest-leverage skill and what separates average brands from breakout ones.

**Recruitment:**
- Cold outreach has diminishing returns (creators get hundreds of requests daily).
- Recruit from existing TikTok affiliate partner networks and creator groups.
- Attract top creators by becoming a brand people want to be associated with.

**Onboarding:**
- Every creator goes through a 3-hour orientation (recorded video + Notion doc).
- Verify they actually completed it by having your brand manager ask specific questions.
- Creators can only add value on top of hooks if they deeply understand your product.

**Community Infrastructure:**
- Build a Discord (or Slack) channel for all creators.
- Have your head of growth and brand manager actively engaged in the community.
- Creators talk to each other — winning hooks and strategies spread organically.
- The system becomes self-sustaining: creators calculate what's working and push it forward themselves.

**Tiered System:**
- Create tiers (Tier 1, 2, 3) so creators can work their way up.
- Set GMV (Gross Merchandising Volume) goals — underperformers get removed.
- People want to feel special. The tier system creates aspiration.

**Incentives Beyond Money:**
- Great commissions and great product are table stakes.
- Prize systems: Rolexes, trips, billboards in Times Square, Coachella invites.
- Only the top performers get exclusive rewards — scarcity drives motivation.
- A creator is only so driven by money. Culture and belonging are the real retention drivers.

### 5. Brand Culture Translation

- As the founder/CEO, personally communicate company culture to creators (quarterly Zoom calls).
- Make creators feel like they are part of the brand, not external contractors.
- The connection between brand leadership and creators is what most companies scaling on TikTok lack.
- Companies maxing out at $100K/month on TikTok are usually missing this human connection layer.

### 6. Consumer Insight & Data Literacy

- Use consumer insight platforms (Meltwater, Epsilon) to understand where you overindex on specific demographics.
- At early stages (0-$20M): use checkout surveys, quarterly customer surveys, Shopify data.
- At scale: invest in paid data (IRI/SPINS for retail, consumer behavior databases).
- Overlay TikTok/Amazon/Shopify/DTC geographic data to find your highest-opportunity markets.
- Every partnership, activation, and content decision should be driven by data on who your consumer actually is.

### 7. Full-Funnel Marketing Thinking

- TikTok is both top-of-funnel (brand awareness) and bottom-of-funnel (TikTok Shop sales).
- The optimal split Neurogum found: ~80% top-of-funnel, ~20% bottom-of-funnel on TikTok.
- Use Google Performance Max to capture search intent generated by TikTok awareness.
- Facebook/Meta becomes a "hygiene" play (~20-30% of digital spend), not the growth engine.
- TikTok awareness lifts ALL channels: Amazon, DTC, retail velocity.
- All roads lead to TikTok: every partnership, PR hit, and activation is designed to generate content that feeds back into the TikTok ecosystem.

### 8. Macro Trend Awareness + Brand-Native Content

- Follow macro trends because the algorithm rewards trend participation.
- But layer your brand's unique story, messaging, and positioning on top of trends.
- The combination of trend-riding + authentic brand storytelling is what scales.

## The Step-by-Step TikTok Growth Playbook

1. **Start posting daily as the founder/leader.** Learn the platform firsthand for 6 months minimum.
2. **Build a hook library.** Track what resonates in a spreadsheet. Test hooks systematically.
3. **Create an SOP/brand bible.** Notion doc, orientation video, talking points — make it thorough.
4. **Recruit creators.** Pull from TAPs, existing networks, and organic applications.
5. **Onboard rigorously.** 3-hour orientation + verification they understand your brand.
6. **Build community.** Discord channel, active brand manager presence, creator-to-creator interaction.
7. **Implement a tier system.** GMV goals, progression paths, exclusive rewards for top performers.
8. **Feed winning content back into the system.** Hooks that work get shared with all creators.
9. **Let the flywheel spin.** Creators self-optimize once they see what's working in the community.
10. **Scale with data.** Use consumer insights to identify partnerships and activations that feed back into TikTok content.

## What NOT to Do

- **Don't rely solely on agencies/TAPs.** They charge retainers + percentage, do the same thing for every brand, and hit saturation.
- **Don't mass-blast samples without follow-up.** Sending 5,000 samples and hoping for videos is the old influencer model. It doesn't work.
- **Don't over-invest in production quality early.** Polished content underperforms raw, authentic creator content in the current TikTok environment.
- **Don't try to sell to everyone.** Go deep and narrow on your target consumer, not wide and shallow.
- **Don't depend on lucky PR moments.** Build systems to capitalize on them when they happen (Rogan mentions, Shark Tank reruns, etc.).

## Critical Mindset Shifts

1. **From "pay to play" to "content to play."** TikTok democratizes reach — good content wins regardless of spend.
2. **From transactional to relational.** Treat creators like team members, not vendors.
3. **From ego-driven hustle to leverage-driven focus.** One high-impact move beats thousands of hours optimizing ad accounts.
4. **From rigid departments to organizational fluidity.** Everyone should understand how their work feeds into TikTok and the broader marketing engine.
5. **From "find what works and stop" to "find what works and compound."** Credibility moments (Shark Tank, Rogan, partnerships) are fuel — keep leveraging them in creator content forever.

## Summary: The 10 Skills to Master

1. **Consistency discipline** — post daily, iterate constantly
2. **Hook writing & testing** — systematic A/B testing of opening lines
3. **Platform literacy** — hands-on TikTok knowledge before delegating
4. **Creator recruitment & onboarding** — rigorous orientation, brand education
5. **Community architecture** — Discord, tiers, culture, belonging
6. **Brand storytelling** — translating your origin, mission, and credibility into content

9. **Partnership evaluation** — using consumer overindex data to choose the right collaborations
10. **Leadership & culture building** — making creators and employees feel part of something bigger than a transaction
